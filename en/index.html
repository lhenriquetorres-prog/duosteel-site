What we build

Three systems (structural steel, light steel frame and precast with reinforced concrete), backed by the same engineering.

System 01

Structural steel

Frames and trusses for long spans: industrial and logistics warehouses, agricultural sheds, mezzanines, roofs and special structures.

  • Clear spans with no intermediate columns
  • In-house structural design and detailing
  • Fabrication with welded and rolled sections
System 02

Light Steel Frame

Light galvanized steel profiles for dry construction: buildings up to five stories, commercial spaces, offices and building envelopes.

  • Clean job site, no rubble, no curing time
  • Thermal and acoustic performance designed per project
  • Works together with structural steel framing
System 03

Precast and reinforced concrete

Precast columns and beams, foundations, slabs and industrial floors in reinforced concrete: the base of hybrid warehouses with steel roofs.

  • Hybrid warehouse: precast columns and steel roof
  • Reinforced concrete industrial floors and loading docks
  • Office blocks integrated with the warehouse

Why steel instead of masonry

The structure is fabricated while the foundations are being built. The job is no longer sequential — the schedule shrinks, and so does the waste.

The weight of the structure comes from calculation, not estimates: the budget is closed before construction starts.
