Frequently asked questions

Do you work throughout Paraguay and Brazil?

Yes. We operate in both countries: we fabricate and erect in Paraguay and Brazil, and we handle the documentation, logistics and customs clearance ourselves. For distant sites, transport and mobilization costs are itemized in the proposal.

What is the difference between structural steel and light steel frame?

Structural steel uses welded or rolled sections to cover long spans — it is the system for warehouses and sheds. Light steel frame uses light galvanized profiles for buildings up to five stories, with dry-built walls and floors. Many projects combine both.

Is the structural design included in the quote?

Yes. The structural design and the foundation design are part of the project scope, with calculations, detailed engineering and the signature of the engineer of record, coordinated with the architectural design.

Do you build the foundations?

Yes, with our own crews. We deliver turnkey: design, foundations, fabrication, erection and a structure ready for use under a single contract.

How long does a steel building project take?

It depends on the floor area and complexity, but the structure is fabricated while the foundations are being built, which shortens the schedule compared with masonry. The fabrication and erection timeline is fixed during detailed engineering, before construction starts.

Do you take part in public tenders?

Yes. We provide the technical specification, quantity takeoff and budget, a cost-loaded schedule and the documents required by the tender terms.

How much does a warehouse cost per m²?

It depends on the clear span, the height and the envelope. With the floor area, the span and the location we will send you an investment range within one business day.
